Free vs. Paid

The free tier is often the riskiest one.

This surprises people: on most consumer AI tools, the free and personal-paid plans are the ones most likely to use your conversations for training — not the free plan being "safer" because you're not paying. Business and enterprise tiers almost universally exclude your data from training by default. Here's how the major tools currently compare.

Tool Personal / Free Plan Business Plan
ChatGPT Trains by default
Must manually opt out in Data Controls		 Excluded by default
Business, Enterprise, Edu, and API
Claude Trains by default
Free, Pro, Max — opt out in Privacy settings		 Excluded by default

Gemini for Google Workspace — stays in your domain

The takeaway: if anyone on your team is using a personal ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini account for work — even just to draft an email or summarize a document — there's a real chance that content is being used to train the next model. That includes anything pasted in: client names, financial figures, contract language, HR issues.

Team Licensing

Why business plans are worth it for growing teams.

Beyond the training question, business-tier plans give you controls that matter as soon as more than one or two people are using AI at work.

Personal / Individual Plans

  • No visibility into what employees are pasting into AI tools
  • No central billing or user management
  • Data may be used for training by default
  • Access doesn't get revoked automatically when someone leaves
Recommended for teams

Team / Business Plans

  • No training on your data, by default
  • Centralized admin console — add, remove, and manage users
  • SSO support (varies by provider and tier)
  • One invoice, one place to manage spend
  • Access is removed the moment someone offboards

Anthropic's Claude Team plan starts around $25/user/month — often cheaper than the personal Pro tier once you account for the privacy and admin controls you gain. Most providers price similarly. For a business handling client data, the math almost always favors the team plan.
